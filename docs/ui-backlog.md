# UI backlog

Things to change in the screens that are agreed but not done yet.

## Bottom bar

Keep it to four destinations, as Cashew does, instead of adding one per
feature:

- **Home**: the money in all accounts, the budgets as charts, a chart of
  the total balance day by day, and the list of everything that happened.
- **Transactions**: a carousel to choose the month, a small header with
  the expenses, the income and the total, the upcoming transactions, and
  those of the chosen month.
- **Budgets**: a card per budget with its name, what is left, a chart and
  how much can be spent per day to use it up on its last day.
- **More**: settings and everything else, including the accounts, the
  goals, subscriptions and scheduled transactions, and editing the
  accounts, categories, titles, budgets and goals.

Today the bar has Transactions, Stats, Budgets and More, and the accounts
are under More. Home is not built. The bar cannot take a fifth
destination, so ask the owner whether Home replaces Stats in the bar
(with the statistics opened from Home) or the statistics stay where they
are.
